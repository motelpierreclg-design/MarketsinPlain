Frequently asked

Can I suggest a topic?

Yes — that's one of the best reasons to reach out. If there's a story in the news you'd like explained in plain English, send it through the form or on Instagram.

How often do new articles come out?

New pieces are published weekly, alongside daily-ish posts on the Instagram account.

Do you take guest contributions?

Not currently, but that may change as the project grows. Reach out if you have something in mind.

I spotted a factual error — what now?

Please flag it through the contact form with a link or source if you have one. Corrections are always welcome and taken seriously.
