About

Written by Pierre

An economics and finance student at UQAM, I spent several years running my own construction businesses before heading back to school. That detour taught me something: most economic concepts that sound complicated aren't — they're just badly explained.

Markets in Plain English grew out of that idea. Each piece takes a current economic or financial story and explains it without jargon and without assuming a background in the subject.
