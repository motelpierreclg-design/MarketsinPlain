PRIVACY

How your information is handled here.

Short version: your email is only used to send you the newsletter, nothing is sold, and you can leave anytime.

Last updated: September 2026

What information is collected

If you subscribe to the newsletter, the only information collected is your email address, submitted voluntarily through the subscribe form. No other personal data is requested or required.

How it's used

Your email address is used solely to send the Markets in Plain newsletter. It is never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or other third parties for marketing purposes.

Who processes it

Newsletter emails are sent through MailerLite, a third-party email service provider that stores subscriber addresses on this site's behalf. MailerLite has its own privacy practices, viewable on their website, and acts as the data processor for this list.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand overall traffic patterns — for example, which articles are read most and roughly where visitors come from. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects general browsing data; it does not collect your name or email address unless you've separately provided it elsewhere.

Unsubscribing

Every newsletter email includes an unsubscribe link. Clicking it removes your address from the list immediately, with no need to contact anyone.

Contact form

Messages sent through the contact page are used only to respond to your inquiry and are not added to the newsletter list unless you separately subscribe.

Questions

For anything not covered here, reach out through the contact page.
